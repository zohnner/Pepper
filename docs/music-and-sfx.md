# Music & SFX — free sourcing

## The score (series identity)

Sparse, wistful, consistent. Solo piano or music-box tones under narration; near-silence for the lift shot. The same 2-3 beds rotate across all episodes — repetition builds identity.

## Free sources (commercial-use safe)

- **YouTube Audio Library** — filter by "attribution not required," download, keep the license note in `docs/licenses/`
- **Pixabay Music** — free for commercial use, no attribution required
- **CapCut built-in library** — cleared for use in CapCut exports (check terms if re-uploading the same file natively elsewhere)
- **TikTok Commercial Music Library** — for in-app discovery sounds layered under the score

## Rules

1. Save every license/attribution note in `docs/licenses/` the day you download the track.
2. Trending sounds are for discovery, not identity — layer them low or use for the first 2 seconds, then let the score take over.
3. Never pull a random MP3 off the internet. A copyright strike can kill the account faster than any algorithm.
4. SFX (drips, chains, lantern flicker): Pixabay SFX or CapCut built-ins, same license rule.
