# Weekly Rhythm — 14 episodes, 2/day

## Sunday — Writing (G-Unit)
Batch-write all 14 scripts from `bible/continuity.md`. Scripts land in `episodes/epNNN/script.md` with prompts and voiceover text. Continuity log updated as episodes are drafted.

## Monday + Tuesday — Visuals (G-Unit)
Generate scene stills with Pepper's reference chained in, then animate each into short clips. Assets land in `episodes/epNNN/assets/`.

## Wednesday — Voice (G-Unit)
Voiceover audio for all 14 episodes, in Pepper's locked voice. Assets land in `episodes/epNNN/assets/`.

## Thursday — Assembly (Zohn, desktop)
CapCut template: clips + voiceover + captions. Export clean masters (no watermarks).

## Daily — Posting (Zohn)
Two slots: morning and evening. Native uploads, scheduled ahead via TikTok scheduler / Meta Business Suite. Never cross-post watermarked video.

## Sunday (10 min) — Review
Which two episodes popped? What did they have in common? Feed it back into next week's scripts.
