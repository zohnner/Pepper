# ep001 — The Debt

**Continuity:** follows ep000 (arrival). Pepper's debt: 47 cranks. Lift cranks earned: 0.

### Hook
"Marlow says I owe him forty-seven cranks. I counted the lift chains myself. There are only twelve."

### Scenes

**Shot 1 — Pepper waking in a puddle, lantern light above, Nib's face upside-down in frame**
Voiceover: "I woke up underground with a headache and a rat going through my pockets."

**Shot 2 — The Night Market, stalls and lanterns, Pepper walking with Nib riding her impatience**
Voiceover: "The kid's name is Nib. He failed to rob me, so now he's my problem. His words."

**Shot 3 — Marlow's Hall, red-gold lanterns, Marlow sliding a brass key across a table**
Voiceover: "Then the possum showed me the lift. One crank per paid debt. Forty-seven debts."

**Shot 4 — The lift: rusted platform, chains, a grate far above leaking a shaft of daylight**
Voiceover: "Twelve chains. I counted. So either the possum can't do math —"

### Complication
Marlow smiles: "Cranks, my dear. Not chains. A crank is whatever I say it is."

### Resolution + new debt
Pepper's first job: deliver a sealed envelope through the flood tunnels by lantern-light. Payment: one crank. She takes the envelope.

### The lift (final 3s, no dialogue)
Marlow's paw on the crank. It doesn't move. He looks at Pepper. Cut.
