# ep001 — Visual Prompts

Style block: "Stylized 3D animation, storybook noir. Warm lantern amber light against deep blue-green shadow. Wet brick, reflective cobblestones, cinematic, highly detailed."
Pepper block: "small grey tabby cat with a torn left ear, a crooked white stripe down her nose, and oversized green eyes, thin street-cat build"

## Shot 1
Pepper lying in a shallow puddle in a dark brick tunnel, just waking, one hanging lantern above her, a scruffy young brown rat with a red bottle-cap helmet leaning into frame upside-down. Slow push-in, water ripples, Pepper's eyes open.

## Shot 2
Pepper walking through a lantern-lit underground market of tiny stalls, the young rat riding on a crate beside her gesturing excitedly, warm amber light, wet cobblestones reflecting the stalls. Slow lateral drift following Pepper.

## Shot 3
A fat sleek possum in a waistcoat of stitched umbrella fabric sliding a brass key across a wooden table toward Pepper, red-gold lanterns, converted cistern hall, velvet drapes. Slow push-in on the key.

## Shot 4
A rusted freight platform on heavy chains in a vertical shaft, a small grate far above leaking one shaft of daylight down through darkness, Pepper small at the edge looking up. Very slow tilt up the chains toward the light.

## The lift (final)
A possum's paw resting on a large iron crank handle, red-gold lantern light, shallow depth of field. The paw tenses. Hold, then cut to black.
