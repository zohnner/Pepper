# ep003 — Old Moss

**Continuity:** follows ep002. Debt: 46 cranks. Lift cranks earned: 1. Pepper seeks counsel, does no job this episode.

### Hook
"There's a turtle under the Mile who remembers when the tunnels were rivers."

### Scenes

**Shot 1 — Pepper and Nib descending ancient stone steps into a grotto of glowing blue-green moss**
Voiceover: "Nib says Old Moss remembers everything. Even the way up."

**Shot 2 — Old Moss: an enormous ancient turtle, shell covered in moss and tiny lanterns, eyes like embers, Pepper small before him**
Voiceover: "I asked him how to get home faster. He laughed for a full minute."

**Shot 3 — Extreme close-up of Moss's ancient eye, Pepper reflected in it**
Voiceover: "Then he said: the lift goes up, little stray. But debts only fall if you stop counting his way. Count what HE counts, not what you see."

**Shot 4 — Pepper frozen mid-step on the stairs, ears up, a shaft of light falling from a grate above**
Voiceover: "On the way back, I heard her. Just once. My name, from above."

### Complication
Moss gives her no job and no shortcut — only a riddle. The way up is still Marlow's lift.

### Resolution + new debt
No cranks earned or paid. Debt stays 46. But Pepper now understands the game: Marlow's counting, not the chains, is the debt.

### The lift (final 3s, no dialogue)
Pepper alone at the base of the lift shaft, looking up. The platform is still. The light is unchanged. Cut.

~80 words.
