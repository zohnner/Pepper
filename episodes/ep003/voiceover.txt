There's a turtle under the Mile who remembers when the tunnels were rivers.

Nib says Old Moss remembers everything. Even the way up.

I asked him how to get home faster. He laughed for a full minute.

Then he said: the lift goes up, little stray. But debts only fall if you stop counting his way. Count what HE counts, not what you see.

On the way back, I heard her. Just once. My name, from above.
