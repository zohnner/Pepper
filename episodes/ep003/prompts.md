# ep003 — Visual Prompts

Style block: "Stylized 3D animation, storybook noir. Warm lantern amber light against deep blue-green shadow. Wet brick, reflective cobblestones, cinematic, highly detailed. VERTICAL 9:16, 1080x1920."
Pepper block: "small grey tabby cat with a torn left ear, a crooked white stripe down her nose, and oversized green eyes, thin street-cat build"

## Shot 1
Pepper and a scruffy young brown rat with a red bottle-cap helmet descending ancient worn stone steps into a vast grotto of glowing blue-green moss, tiny lanterns dotting the walls. Slow downward drift, moss glimmers.

## Shot 2
An enormous ancient turtle with a moss-covered shell dotted with tiny lit lanterns, eyes like glowing embers, Pepper small before him in the grotto, Nib hiding behind her leg. Moss blinks slowly, lantern flames flicker, very slow push-in.

## Shot 3
Extreme close-up of the ancient turtle's ember eye, Pepper's small reflection visible in it. Hold, then very slow zoom into the reflection.

## Shot 4
Pepper frozen mid-step on stone stairs, ears perked up, a single shaft of light falling from a grate far above, dust motes drifting. Hold on Pepper, dust drifts in the light.

## The lift (final)
Pepper small at the base of a vast vertical lift shaft, the rusted platform still on its chains, one shaft of daylight above, unchanged. Very slow tilt up toward the distant grate. Cut to black.
