The envelope was sealed. I told myself that meant it wasn't my business.

Marlow's first job: carry it through the flood tunnels. One crank. Simple.

Nib came anyway. Said the tunnels take a toll. I told him I don't pay tolls.

The envelope was an eviction notice. For a family of mice.

The mother thanked me. Said at least the mayor sent someone kind. I earned my first crank tonight. It felt like stealing.
