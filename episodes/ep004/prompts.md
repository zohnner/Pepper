# ep004 — Visual Prompts

Style block: "Stylized 3D animation, storybook noir. Warm lantern amber light against deep blue-green shadow. Wet brick, reflective cobblestones, cinematic, highly detailed. VERTICAL 9:16, 1080x1920."
Pepper block: "small grey tabby cat with a torn left ear, a crooked white stripe down her nose, and oversized green eyes, thin street-cat build"

## Shot 1
Pepper straining with both paws at a huge iron valve wheel in a stone surge chamber, water spraying from the pipes, her fur soaked, lantern light flickering. Valve turns slowly, water sprays, Pepper strains, slow push-in.

## Shot 2
A scruffy young brown rat with a red bottle-cap helmet waist-deep in swirling water, bailing with the helmet, grinning. Bobbing, splashing, helmet scoops, lateral drift.

## Shot 3
A fat sleek possum in a waistcoat of stitched umbrella fabric reading from a long unrolling paper ledger, Pepper dripping wet on a rug before him, red-gold lanterns, velvet drapes. Slow push-in on the ledger, the possum's paw taps it.

## Shot 4
Close-up of Pepper's wet face, flat unimpressed stare, water dripping from her torn ear, red-gold lantern light. Hold, water drips.

## The lift (final)
A possum's paw resting on a large iron crank handle, red-gold lantern light, shallow depth of field. The paw tenses. The crank does not move. Hold, then cut to black.
