# Pepper — Character Bible (LOCKED)

## Identity

- **Name:** Pepper
- **Species:** Domestic shorthair tabby, female, small for her age (~2 years)
- **Build:** Thin from street life, light on her feet, tail a little too long

## Fixed visual anchors (repeat in EVERY visual prompt)

1. Torn left ear (a V-shaped notch)
2. Crooked white stripe down the bridge of her nose, veering left
3. Oversized green eyes

If a generated image is missing any of these, it gets regenerated. These three marks are what make her recognizable at a glance.

## Coloring

Grey tabby stripes over a pale cream underbelly. White socks on the front paws only. Pink nose with one grey spot on the left edge.

## Personality

- Dry and sarcastic out loud; every feeling stated as its opposite
- Hyper-observant — notices exits, debts, and lies before anyone finishes talking
- **Flaw:** pushes away anyone who gets close. The story punishes this weekly
- Brave in bursts, then immediately annoyed at herself for caring

## The wound (the heartstring engine)

Pepper had a girl once — maybe six years old, shared tuna straight from the can, braided Pepper's whisker-tufts when she thought no one was looking. One thunderstorm night: a slammed door, a wrong turn down an alley, a storm drain with a missing grate. Pepper has been underground ever since. She does not talk about the girl. She thinks about her every episode.

## Voice (spoken)

Dry, female, slightly raspy. Sarcastic on the surface, soft underneath. Never cute — the comedy comes from how seriously she takes absurd situations, and the emotion lands because she never asks for sympathy.

## What Pepper never does

- Never begs or acts helpless to earn pity
- Never trusts Marlow, no matter how charming he is
- Never leaves Nib behind, no matter how loudly she complains about him
