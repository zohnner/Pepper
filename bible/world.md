# The Drowned Mile — World Bible (LOCKED)

## Overview

A tunnel city beneath the surface world, built in old storm drains, subway bones, and forgotten brickwork. Lantern-lit, damp, alive. It runs on a barter of favors, and everything has a price.

## Look and feel

- Mossy brick tunnels, wet cobblestones, hanging lanterns (warm amber against deep shadow)
- Water everywhere: drips, puddles, slow canals through the main tunnels
- Reflections matter — wet stone doubles every light source
- Districts have color temperatures: the market is amber, the flood tunnels are cold blue-green, Marlow's hall is red-gold

## Key locations

- **The Lift** — the only way back to the surface. A rusted freight platform on chains, controlled by Marlow. Rises one crank per paid debt. The light from the grate above is the series' recurring final image.
- **Marlow's Hall** — a converted cistern, red-gold lanterns, velvet stolen from somewhere. Where debts are negotiated.
- **The Night Market** — stalls run by rats, raccoons, pigeons. Barter only. Nib works the crowd here.
- **The Flood Tunnels** — cold, dangerous, fastest route between districts. Episode workhorse for chases and deliveries.
- **Old Moss's Alcove** — a dry brick nook behind a waterfall grate. Smells like tea. The only quiet place in the Mile.

## Rules of the Mile

1. Debts are public — everyone knows what everyone owes
2. The lift moves for paid debts only, one crank at a time, witnessed
3. No one goes up without Marlow's key
4. Surface things (a bottle cap, a hair ribbon) are currency-grade valuables
5. Don't drink the canal water. Nobody explains why.
