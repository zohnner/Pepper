# Cast Bible (LOCKED)

## Marlow — the mayor

- **Species:** Possum, large, immaculately groomed (suspiciously so)
- **Look:** Fat and sleek, waistcoat made from a stitched-together umbrella, pocket watch that doesn't work
- **Personality:** Charming the way a locked door is charming. Never raises his voice. Every favor is a contract.
- **Role:** Controls the lift. Sets Pepper's jobs. The debt is always one job longer than she expects.
- **Voice direction:** Smooth, unhurried, amused. Calls Pepper "my dear contractor."

## Nib — the kid

- **Species:** Rat, young, all elbows and ears
- **Look:** Scruffy brown fur, a stolen red bottle cap worn as a helmet, moves at double speed
- **Personality:** Hyperactive pickpocket with a hero complex. Adopted Pepper as his big sister on day one; she has filed no fewer than a dozen verbal objections, all ignored.
- **Role:** Comic relief, heart, and the reason Pepper keeps getting into worse trouble — she'll risk anything for him while denying it.
- **Voice direction:** Fast, breathless, earnest.

## Old Moss — the oracle

- **Species:** Turtle, very old, moss growing on his shell (hence the name)
- **Look:** Ancient, slow-blinking, lives in the alcove behind the waterfall grate
- **Personality:** Speaks only in riddles and half-answers. Might be the only one who knows how Pepper really got down here — and why the drain grate was missing that night.
- **Role:** Mythology engine. Doles out one true thing per visit, wrapped in three confusing ones.
- **Voice direction:** Slow, gravelly, each word weighed.

## The girl (unseen)

- Never shown clearly. A small hand, a laugh, tuna from the can. Appears only in Pepper's flashes — brief, warm, gone before it settles. The audience should ache every time.
