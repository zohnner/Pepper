# Voice & Visual Style (LOCKED)

## Visual style

Stylized 3D animation, storybook noir. The reference is a children's book illustrated by someone who loves rainy nights.

- **Light:** warm lantern amber vs. deep blue-green shadow. Every frame should have both.
- **Texture:** wet brick, dripping water, reflective cobblestones, soft fur detail on Pepper
- **Camera:** slow push-ins and lateral drifts. Intimate, patient. Let the mood do work the plot doesn't have to.
- **Color rule:** the surface world (flashes of the girl) is the only place that gets daylight — warm, overexposed, brief. The Mile never gets daylight.

## Pepper render spec (append to every visual prompt)

"small grey tabby cat with a torn left ear, a crooked white stripe down her nose, and oversized green eyes, thin street-cat build"

## Voiceover style

- Pepper narrates in first person, past tense, like she's telling you what happened
- Dry observations first, real feeling slipped in sideways
- ~60-90 words per episode (30-45 seconds at natural pace)
- Never explains the joke. Never asks for sympathy.

## Episode formula (fixed)

1. **Cold open (0-3s):** the job, stated flat. Hook in the first line.
2. **Complication (midpoint):** the job goes wrong in a way only the Mile could arrange
3. **Small win + new debt (late):** Pepper succeeds at the job, Marlow finds the catch
4. **The lift (final 3s):** one crank. The light from above a fraction closer. Cut.

## Music direction

Sparse and wistful. Solo piano or music-box tones under narration, gone quiet for the lift shot. Trending sounds may be layered for discovery, but the score is the identity.
